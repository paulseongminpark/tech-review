**Why it matters:** India is emerging as a third global AI infrastructure node alongside the US and China. This isn't just investment — it's compute, connectivity, and standard-setting bundled together.

- $15B infrastructure commitment — gigawatt-scale compute + international subsea cable gateway

**What's next:** Whether India can position itself as a genuine AI infrastructure hub will be decided in 2026.

The University of Arizona hosted an AI Health Innovation Symposium, putting responsible AI principles in clinical settings on the public agenda.

**Why it matters:** The conversation has moved from "can AI work in healthcare?" to "who is accountable when it does?" Rural and underserved access gaps are now formal agenda items.

- Medical research + clinical care AI integration frameworks discussed
