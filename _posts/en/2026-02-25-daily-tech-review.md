## 1. Anthropic Accelerates Enterprise Automation with Specialized Agent Plugins for Finance, Legal, and HR

Anthropic officially launched pre-configured agent plugins for the Claude Cowork platform on February 24. Specialized agents for finance, legal, HR, and engineering teams automate repetitive tasks such as market analysis, financial modeling, job posting creation, and contract review.

**Why it matters:** This launch confronts the frustration of 2025 being declared the "year of agents" yet falling short of expectations, as diagnosed by Anthropic's Chief Product Officer Matt Piccolella as a failure of "inadequate approaches." The key turning point is the pre-configured plugin approach, which reduces IT infrastructure complexity and enables rapid deployment.

- The finance plugin comes standard with competitor analysis, financial modeling, and market research data flows, while the HR plugin automates job postings, onboarding, and offer letters.
- Integration with new enterprise connectors like Gmail, DocuSign, and Clay strengthens connectivity with existing corporate systems.
- Organization-specific workflows and skills can be customized via a centralized admin portal, meeting the IT governance needs of large enterprises.

**What's next:** Anthropic is expected to significantly expand its enterprise customer base with this plugin system, and the market assesses it as materializing the threat of replacing traditional SaaS structures.

## 2. OpenAI Strengthens Enterprise Implementation by Forming Frontier Alliance with McKinsey, BCG, Accenture, and Capgemini

OpenAI officially announced a multi-year "Frontier Alliances" partnership with four major global consulting firms on February 24. McKinsey and Boston Consulting Group handle strategy, operating models, and change management, while Accenture and Capgemini manage technical implementation and data architecture modernization.

**Why it matters:** Just weeks after OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar highlighted "practical adoption" as a key 2026 priority in her January blog, this reflects the market's recognition that lack of implementation expertise is the real bottleneck, beyond AI model performance. Partnering with consulting firms incorporates the lesson that high organizational change management is essential to avoid high failure rates in technology adoption.

- McKinsey plans to support leadership teams in priority setting, operating model redesign, and embedding intelligence into daily operations.
- Accenture specializes in data architecture modernization and expanding secure agent deployments.
- Capgemini handles integration with existing core technology systems and establishing consistent operational processes.

**What's next:** Despite Anthropic's existing partnerships with Deloitte and Accenture, OpenAI will leverage the consulting firm network to compete for enterprise contracts, which is expected to stimulate SaaS companies (Salesforce, ServiceNow, Workday) to accelerate their agent platform strategies.

**Source:** [OpenAI allies with 4 big consulting giants as the agentic enterprise battle heats up](https://siliconangle.com/2026/02/23/openai-allies-4-big-consulting-giants-agentic-enterprise-battle-heats/)

## 3. Goldman Sachs Begins Full Operations of Transaction Accounting and Customer Onboarding Automation Using Anthropic Claude

After six months of joint development of autonomous agents based on Anthropic's Claude model, Goldman Sachs has entered the phase of executing transaction accounting, customer due diligence, and compliance verification with "digital collaborators." Goldman CIO Marco Argenti described them as a "new kind of employee" operating 24/7 without desks or lunch breaks.

**Why it matters:** The deployment of fully autonomous agents in core back-office processes by Wall Street's largest investment bank signals the financial industry's shift from the AI "assistant" phase to the "transaction authority" phase. Amid CEO David Solomon's directive to suppress headcount growth, Goldman officially frames agents as "capability expansion," foreshadowing vendor cost reductions and workforce reconfiguration.

- Upon transaction occurrence, the agent automatically collects necessary documents, parses data, and applies Goldman’s internal accounting rules.
- Goldman's tech executives expressed astonishment at this performance, which became the trigger for expanding beyond the tech department into core business operations.
- Future expansions could include employee monitoring and automated investment pitching, with third-party vendor contracts potentially under review as technology matures.

**What's next:** Goldman Sachs' multi-year AI reorganization plan will manifest as imminent workforce restructuring (more hiring freezes than direct layoffs), and similar-scale financial institutions (Lloyds' £100m+ agentic AI value creation plan, BBVA, Cisco, T-Mobile's early Frontier adoption) are expected to follow the same path.

**Source:** Goldman Sachs taps Anthropic's Claude to automate, [Goldman and Anthropic develop AI agents - eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/goldman-sachs-anthropic-building-ai-agents-banking)
