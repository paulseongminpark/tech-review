## 1. [Phil Spencer Retires, Asha Sharma Promoted to Microsoft Gaming CEO]

Microsoft Gaming head Phil Spencer announced his retirement on February 20, 2026. Internal executive Asha Sharma was immediately appointed as the successor CEO.

**Why it matters:** The leadership change in Microsoft's Xbox division marks a turning point in accelerating AI integration in the gaming industry. The end of Phil Spencer's 20-year career is interpreted as a signal of strategic reorganization.

- Phil Spencer joined Microsoft in 2008 and served for over 10 years from overseeing Xbox Live to Gaming CEO.
- Asha Sharma is a Microsoft veteran who led Xbox Cloud Gaming and Game Pass strategies.
- Microsoft stock dipped slightly right after the retirement announcement, but long-term impacts are unconfirmed.(https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/microsoft-gaming-head-phil-spencer-retires-insider-asha-sharma-takes-over-4517337)

**What's next:** Under Sharma's leadership, Xbox is expected to focus on enhancing AI-based gaming experiences.

## 2. [Microsoft Unveils Maia 200 AI Chip, Directly Competing with Google, Amazon, Nvidia]

Microsoft announced the Maia 200 AI chip on January 26, 2026, entering the AI inference market with its own silicon and explicitly targeting Google, Amazon, and Nvidia.

**Why it matters:** The Maia 200, successor to Maia 100, is a weapon for reducing AI infrastructure costs. It signals shifts in the cloud market amid intensifying chip wars among big tech.

- The Maia 200 features over 100 billion transistors and achieves 10 petaflops performance at 4-bit precision.
- 8-bit performance reaches 5 petaflops, more than doubling efficiency compared to the previous model.
- Microsoft is pushing independent large AI training and inference, such as OpenAI models, with Maia.

**What's next:** Large-scale deployment in the first half of 2026 could secure a competitive edge in Azure AI service speed.

## 3. [Nvidia Invests Additional $2.8 Billion in CoreWeave to Strengthen AI Chip Supply Chain]

Nvidia made an additional $2.8 billion investment in AI cloud company CoreWeave on January 26, 2026, securing mass supply of H100 and H200 chips.

**Why it matters:** CoreWeave is an AI training market fast-grower reliant exclusively on Nvidia chips. This investment maximizes Nvidia's benefits from the AI boom.

- Post-investment, Nvidia holds a key stake in CoreWeave, valued at $19 billion overall.
- CoreWeave is projected to grow from $1.9 billion in 2025 revenue to $8 billion in 2026.
- Coupled with China approval for H200 chip imports, Nvidia forecasts $50 billion in AI segment revenue.

**What's next:** CoreWeave's expansion will surge Nvidia chip demand, making strong Q2 2026 earnings inevitable.
