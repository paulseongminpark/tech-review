## 1. IBM Stock's Worst Drop in 25 Years - Caught Off Guard by Anthropic's COBOL Tool Announcement

Anthropic announced on February 23 that its Claude code tool can modernize COBOL legacy code, causing IBM's stock to plummet 13.2% and evaporate $4 billion in market value in an instant. This marks the worst single-day loss since the dot-com collapse in October 2000, revealing investors' fundamental concerns about IBM's long-term revenue model centered on its mainframe business.[1][3][5]

**Why it matters:** IBM's mainframe business still generates the highest annual revenue, but the possibility that AI could drastically reduce the cost of migrating decades-old COBOL code led investors to worry about long-term value erosion. As of 2021 estimates, global companies operate about 250 billion lines of COBOL code, which handles 95% of U.S. ATM transactions and 80% of credit card payments.

- IBM closed at $223.35, down 13.2% from the previous day following Anthropic's COBOL announcement, with market cap decreasing by about $4 billion[1][3]
- Cumulative decline over the next 12 months exceeds 24%, recording the worst monthly drop in history in February alone (since 1968)[3]
- IBM achieved its highest mainframe profit in 20 years last month, but the potential threat from AI tools is under fundamental evaluation

**What's next:** IBM has been pursuing a COBOL modernization strategy with its own Watsonx Code Assistant since 2023, but the market anticipates a reevaluation of long-term negotiations as Anthropic's broader AI ecosystem may seize competitiveness.[1]

## 2. OpenAI Investor Loyalty Collapses - At Least 12 VCs Simultaneously Invest in Anthropic's $30 Billion Funding Round

Anthropic closed a massive $30 billion funding round in mid-February, with at least 12 direct OpenAI investors including Founders Fund, Iconiq, Insight Partners, and Sequoia Capital also investing in Anthropic simultaneously, and even a BlackRock affiliate joining, creating cracks in Silicon Valley's structural investment principles. In particular, BlackRock's Adebayo Ogunlesi, serving as an OpenAI board member, led to a contradictory situation where a BlackRock affiliate fund invested in Anthropic.

**Why it matters:** Traditionally, venture capital firms maintained investment restrictions in direct competitive relationships between portfolio companies, but this convention is quietly disappearing in the face of the AI industry's record growth and astronomical fundraising scales. OpenAI requested in 2024 that investors not invest in rival companies like Anthropic, xAI, and Safe Superintelligence, but investors ignored it.

- Top Silicon Valley VCs such as Sequoia Capital, Founders Fund, and Iconiq directly invested in both OpenAI and Anthropic
- Amid OpenAI's ongoing final stage of a $100 billion new funding round, Anthropic's $30 billion proceeding deepened investor diversification
- Conflicts of interest due to board overlaps are subject to legal review, but investors claim "no issue without board seats" to evade regulation

**What's next:** Explicit conflict-of-interest clauses are expected to be added to future AI investment contracts, and restrictions on investors' access to confidential information are anticipated to be discussed.

## 3. Anthropic Detects Large-Scale Claude Model Distillation by Chinese AI Labs - Illegal Distillation with 16 Million Queries

On February 23, Anthropic detected and disclosed that three Chinese AI labs—DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, and MiniMax—accessed Claude using about 24,000 fake accounts to generate 16 million queries for illegal model distillation. They targeted Claude's most differentiated capabilities such as reasoning, tool use, and coding, with DeepSeek even extracting methods to bypass censorship on politically sensitive queries.

**Why it matters:** Model distillation is a legitimate AI development technique, but using a competitor's model without permission as training data is considered intellectual property infringement and a national security threat. The fact that Chinese companies can replicate U.S. leading models' capabilities in just a few months supports arguments that undermine the effectiveness of AI chip export controls. This disclosure is expected to directly influence the U.S. administration's AI chip export policy decisions toward China.

- DeepSeek: Extracted reasoning capabilities and censorship bypass methods with over 150,000 queries, evading detection with synchronized traffic
- Moonshot AI: Over 3.4 million queries targeting agent reasoning, tool use, and computer-using agent development
- MiniMax: Over 13 million queries extracting agent coding capabilities, switching half of traffic to a new model within 24 hours of launch

**What's next:** Anthropic is enhancing API traffic detection, behavioral fingerprinting, and distillation attack pattern identification, and plans to share technical indicators with the U.S. government and cloud providers to urge industry-wide responses.
