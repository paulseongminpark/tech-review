## Today in One Line
Anthropic announced 1M token context at standard pricing and a $100 million partner network investment, while Mistral unveiled a 675B parameter MoE model, accelerating competition in AI inference costs and enterprise deployment.

## 1. Claude 1M Token Context at Standard Pricing — Complete Elimination of Long-Context Premium

Anthropic began offering 1M (million) token context for Claude Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 at standard pricing on March 13. Previously, requests exceeding 200K tokens incurred a 2x surcharge, but now uniform pricing applies across the entire 1M window.[2]

**Why it matters:** Long-context usage is now cost-effective for developers handling large codebases and extended agent sessions. This represents a turning point that can fundamentally improve the economics of AI agent-based software development.[2]

- Opus 4.6: Unified $5/$5/$25 per million tokens pricing across the entire 1M window, premium charges removed
- Sonnet 4.6: Unified $3/$3/$15 per million tokens pricing, with 900K token requests billed at the same rate as 9K token requests[2]
- Opus 4.6 provided by default for Claude Code Max, Team, and Enterprise users with no additional cost[2]

**What's next:** Expect intensified long-context pricing competition from other AI providers and accelerated adoption of AI agent-based automation workflows.

**Source:** Anthropic announced 1M context GA at standard pricing[2]

## 2. Anthropic Invests $100 Million in Claude Partner Network  Expanding Enterprise Deployment Ecosystem

Anthropic committed $100 million in early 2026 funding to support partner companies adopting Claude. These funds are allocated to education, technical support, and joint marketing, with plans to expand the partner-facing team by 5x.

**Why it matters:** Partner ecosystems are becoming critical in enterprise customer acquisition competition, and Claude Code's annual revenue already exceeds $2.5 billion, driving SaaS industry transformation. This investment bridges the gap from proof-of-concept to production in enterprise AI deployment.

- Claude Code annual revenue exceeds $2.5 billion, doubling since January 1; business subscriptions increased 4x
- Customers spending over $100K annually increased 7x in the past year; over 500 customers spending $1M+ annually
- Accenture opened Claude access to 30,000 employees, Cognizant to 350,000, and Infosys established a dedicated center

**What's next:** Competing AI providers (OpenAI, Google) are expected to intensify enterprise market competition through similar partner investments, with Anthropic's multi-cloud availability strategy across AWS/GCP/Azure becoming a key competitive factor in partner adoption.

## 3. Mistral 3 Released — Open MoE Model Up to 675B Parameters Under Apache 2.0 License

Mistral AI released the Mistral 3 model family, with the flagship Mistral Large 3 employing a mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture with 41B active parameters out of 675B total parameters. All models are distributed under Apache 2.0 license with NVIDIA GPU optimization included.

**Why it matters:** Open-source AI model performance is converging with major closed models (GPT, Claude), diversifying model choices for enterprises and developer communities. Open-source MoE architecture implementation increases inference efficiency, expanding possibilities for edge deployment and cost optimization.

- Mistral Large 3 was trained from scratch on 3,000 NVIDIA H200 GPUs and achieved 2nd place in LMArena open-source reasoning model rankings
- Ministral 3 (3B, 8B, 14B) offers the best performance-to-cost ratio among similarly-sized models with significantly reduced generated token volume
- Collaboration with NVIDIA, vLLM, and Red Hat enables efficient inference on Blackwell NVL72 and single-node A100/H100 configurations

**What's next:** Mistral 3's diverse size options (3B–675B) will drive rapid downstream adoption across edge devices to data centers, while strengthened NVIDIA optimization ecosystems will intensify hardware-software integration competition.
