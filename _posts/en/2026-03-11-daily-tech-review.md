## Today in One Line
From clinical trial AI to customer interaction automation, healthcare, finance, and retail are simultaneously entering large-scale AI adoption, accelerating business model transformation.

---

## 1. Evinova Signs AI Clinical Trial Platform Agreements with Three Global Pharma Companies

On March 3rd, clinical trial AI platform Evinova signed separate agreements with Astellas, Bristol Myers Squibb (BMS), and AstraZeneca to apply AI to optimize clinical trial design and document management in drug development. This represents a clear signal that global big pharma is proactively adopting AI-driven clinical development.

**Why it matters:** Clinical trials represent a 70–80% bottleneck in drug development, and AI-driven efficiency directly translates to shortened development timelines and cost reduction. Simultaneously, nearly concurrent contracts from multiple pharma companies signal that this market has already moved beyond the pilot phase into actual operational deployment.

- The combined operational data from three pharma companies will be leveraged to develop industry benchmarks, strengthening Evinova's platform market dominance.
- Clinical trial design automation and document management automation are key areas capable of shortening development cycles by several months.
- This trend is likely to lead to additional partnership announcements in the first half of 2026, progressing toward standardization competition in the clinical operations AI market.

**What's next:** Evinova and competitors are expected to enter M&A and additional fundraising competition for standard platform status in clinical operations AI. The clinical operations AI market is projected to reach tens of billions of dollars by the end of 2026.

**Source:** [Evinova signs AI clinical-trial deals with Astellas, Bristol Myers Squibb, and AstraZeneca](https://www.bioxconomy.com/clinical-and-research/evinova-inks-ai-deals-with-astellas-bristol-myers-squibb-and-astrazeneca-for-clinical-trials)

## 2. Block CEO Jack Dorsey Implements Layoffs of 4,000+ Employees Citing AI Adoption

In early March, Block CEO Jack Dorsey announced a restructuring that eliminated over 4,000 employees, approximately 7% of the company's workforce. Dorsey publicly justified this by stating that "small teams armed with AI tools can operate far more efficiently than large organizations."

**Why it matters:** A CEO-level explicit declaration that "AI adoption is a direct reason for layoffs" signals that corporate leadership has accepted labor replacement through AI as a core business strategy. This marks the point where the AI-driven workforce reduction trend from late 2025 has become mainstream management strategy.

- Block accelerated AI investment from late 2025, but this is the first time a CEO has officially linked layoffs to AI adoption.
- Similar AI-driven restructuring is underway at major tech and AI companies including Meta, Google, Amazon, and Anthropic.
- The market is evaluating this not as cost-cutting but as "competitive strengthening," with some positive market reaction evident in financial markets.

**What's next:** Additional layoff announcements from Fortune 500 companies are expected during Q2, with each company presenting "AI productivity improvement rates" as justification for workforce reduction scale. This will likely expand AI ethics, retraining policy, and labor protection debates to the government level.

**Source:** [Jack Dorsey links major workforce cuts to aggressive AI adoption](https://www.marketingprofs.com/opinions/2026/54379/ai-update-march-6-2026-ai-news-and-views-from-the-past-week)

## 3. By End of 2026, 37% of UK Shoppers Will Use AI Agents as First Choice for Search and Purchase

According to Braze's 2026 Customer Experience Report (CER 2026 Report), 14% of UK consumers currently interact with brands through AI agents, with this figure projected to increase nearly threefold to 37% by year-end. Consumers prefer AI as a mediator for "fast, smart, and personalized" shopping experiences over Google search or brand websites.

**Why it matters:** This signals the beginning of structural transformation in retail customer touchpoints. The shift from a model dominated by search engine optimization (SEO) and direct brand visits to an AI intermediary model is occurring in real-time, fundamentally restructuring advertising, recommendations, and personalization strategies. Additionally, 60% of shoppers expect better deals (40%) and reduced decision-making friction (20%) from AI experiences, signifying fundamental change in customer acquisition cost (CAC) structures.

- While specific to the UK market, similar trajectories are expected in the US and major European markets, with potential 5–10 percentage point increases every three months.
- Amazon and eBay have already implemented restrictions explicitly blocking third-party AI agents from OpenAI, Google, and others on their platforms, which may escalate into future legal disputes.
- Retail media networks and advertisers are competing for product exposure and recommendation algorithm access on AI shopping agents, signaling the emergence of new fee models (agent-in-the-loop advertising).

**What's next:** Each retail platform and e-commerce company is expected to announce AI shopping agent launches or feature enhancements within Q2, with advertisers' digital marketing budgets shifting at scale from search advertising to agent-based recommendation advertising. Simultaneously, government regulators are projected to intensify oversight of AI agent transparency, bias, and consumer data usage.

**Source:** [AI could drive 37% of customer interactions by end of 2026](https://www.cxnetwork.com/artificial-intelligence/news/ai-could-drive-37-of-customer-interactions-by-end-of-2026)
