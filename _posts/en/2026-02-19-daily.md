## World Labs Raises $1 Billion for Spatial Intelligence Development

Fei-Fei Li's World Labs closed a **$1 billion funding round** to accelerate development of "spatial intelligence" systems that process visual data from physical environments for advanced reasoning[4]. The round included major investors such as AMD, Nvidia, Autodesk (which committed $200 million and will serve as an adviser), Emerson Collective, Fidelity Management & Research Company, and Sea[4]. World Labs is developing world models—systems that can understand and reason about spatial environments—positioning the startup alongside Google DeepMind and other competitors in this emerging AI category, with potential applications in augmented reality, virtual reality, and robotics[4].

## Ineffable Intelligence Raises $1 Billion at $4 Billion Valuation

Ineffable Intelligence, a London-based startup led by former Google DeepMind principal research scientist David Silver, is **raising $1 billion in funding** with Sequoia Capital leading the round and potential participation from Alphabet, Nvidia, and Microsoft[2]. The startup, founded in November 2025, has no product yet but plans to build on Silver's reinforcement learning expertise from his work on AlphaGo and AlphaZero at DeepMind[2]. The $1 billion raise at a reported $4 billion valuation reflects investor confidence in Silver's track record, though the company's specific product direction and competitive positioning remain undisclosed[2].

**Source**: https://siliconangle.com/2026/02/18/new-ai-startup-ineffable-intelligence-reportedly-raising-1b-funding-round/

## Temporal Completes $300 Million Series D for AI Agent Infrastructure

AI agent startup Temporal closed a **$300 million Series D funding round** led by a16z to build platform infrastructure addressing reliability challenges in complex, long-running AI agent processes[5]. The company serves major clients including OpenAI, Snap, Netflix, and JPMorgan Chase, positioning itself as critical infrastructure for enterprise AI deployments[5]. This funding reflects growing market recognition that AI agents require robust orchestration and reliability layers as they move into production environments across industries[5].
