## Anthropic's Massive $30B Series G at $380B Valuation Signals Unprecedented AI Scale-Up
Anthropic, the AI research lab behind the Claude models with a strong focus on safety, announced a staggering $30 billion Series G funding round on February 12, 2026, valuing the company at $380 billion—marking one of the largest private funding rounds ever. The round drew over 30 top-tier investors including Founders Fund, Coatue, and Nvidia, underscoring continued confidence in Anthropic's ability to compete with OpenAI amid intensifying AI arms races. This mega-round positions Anthropic to aggressively expand compute resources and talent, potentially accelerating breakthroughs in safe superintelligence while highlighting how AI leaders are commanding capital at levels dwarfing traditional tech unicorns.[1]

## AI Agent Platform Temporal Lands $300M Series D from a16z to Tackle Reliability Hurdles
Temporal, a startup building infrastructure for reliable AI agents in complex, long-running processes, closed a $300 million Series D round on February 18, 2026, led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z). The funding targets core challenges in AI agent deployment, with existing customers like OpenAI, Snap, Netflix, and JPMorgan Chase already relying on its platform for mission-critical workflows. This investment reflects surging demand for robust AI orchestration tools as enterprises scale agentic systems, positioning Temporal as a key enabler in the shift from experimental AI to production-grade applications.[4]

## Runway Boosts Media AI Ambitions with $315M Series E at $5.3B Valuation
Video generation leader Runway raised $315 million in a Series E round announced February 10, 2026, valuing the company at $5.3 billion, with General Atlantic leading alongside Nvidia, Fidelity, and Felicis. The funding comes amid Runway's rapid growth in AI-driven media tools that empower creators to produce cinematic content from text prompts, fueling adoption in film, advertising, and social media. As one of the earliest mega-rounds of 2026, it signals investor bets on generative media as the next frontier for AI monetization, especially with hardware giants like Nvidia backing multimodal models.[1]
