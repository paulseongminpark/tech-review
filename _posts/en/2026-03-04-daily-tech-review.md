## 1. OpenAI Signs $200 Million AI Contract with U.S. Department of Defense — Turning Point for Government Agencies Adopting Business AI

OpenAI has signed a $200 million contract with the U.S. Department of Defense (Department of Defense), beginning operations of advanced AI systems in a classified environment equipped with cloud deployment-based safety measures. This contract was concluded right after negotiations with Anthropic broke down, and it is interpreted as a signal presenting new standards for government AI adoption.

**Why it matters:** The introduction of enterprise AI in the defense sector serves as the starting gun to trigger large-scale adoption by private companies. By explicitly stating three red lines in the contract conditions—"no mass surveillance," "no fully autonomous weapons," and "no high-risk automated decisions"—OpenAI is establishing a governance model for future government-enterprise AI collaborations.

- Includes conditions for cloud-only deployment and retention of OpenAI's proprietary safety stack: Establishes a structure allowing companies to enter government usage environments while maintaining control over AI safety
- OpenAI's contract-based response in preparation for Anthropic's supply chain risk designation: U.S. government acknowledges differing governance options per company, effectively starting coexistence of multiple AI governance models
- Sequential adoption of OpenAI models by U.S. federal agencies ahead of schedule: If expanded beyond the Department of Defense to other agencies, rapid expansion of the government AI adoption market is anticipated

**What's next:** Over the next 6 months, removal of Anthropic's existing Department of Defense systems is expected to proceed, while competition among other U.S. federal agencies to adopt OpenAI, Google, or xAI models will accelerate.

**Source:** OpenAI - Our agreement with the Department of Defense

## 2. LiveRamp Live-Deploys Agentic AI Agents from Newton Research and SemantIQ — Marketing Workflow Automation Enters Full Operations

On March 3, 2026, LiveRamp live-deployed AI agents from Newton Research and SemantIQ (SemanticIQ) on its data collaboration platform, enabling marketing teams to automatically execute audience building, cross-media measurement, and spend optimization without manual work. Just two months after the January agent licensing commitment, it has moved beyond pilot to production deployment, signifying entry into the practical operations phase of marketing automation.

**Why it matters:** As the first case of the marketing industry fully adopting agentic AI, it becomes a watershed moment for ROI validation. Unlike traditional RPA (Robotic Process Automation), agents based on natural language interfaces autonomously execute complex multi-channel decisions, demonstrating the need to reallocate marketing team analysts and strategists.

- Newton Research agent: Instantly derives measurement statistics via natural language queries on LiveRamp Cross-Media Intelligence data, eliminating existing analyst query writing time
- SemantIQ agent: Enables medical and life sciences marketing teams to automatically build and activate physician audiences within LiveRamp cleanrooms, with parallel processing of data licensing and compliance reviews
- Enhanced lookalike modeling: Applies 1st-, 2nd-, and 3rd-party data across channels + single identity-based control groups to ensure consistency in cross-channel performance measurement

**What's next:** LiveRamp plans to expand onboarding of additional partner agents (audience planning, segmentation, optimization), and as trust in agent autonomous execution by marketing teams increases, an average reduction of 20-40 hours of repetitive work per quarter is expected.

**Source:** LiveRamp - Agentic AI Upgrades for Smarter Growth, Planning, and Measurement

## 3. RadNet Acquires Paris-Based Radiology AI Company Gleamer for 230 Million Euros — Accelerating Medical Imaging AI Integration

RadNet has acquired European radiology AI leader Gleamer for 230 million euros (approximately $270 million) and integrated it into its subsidiary DeepHealth, building an integrated radiology AI portfolio spanning over 25 clinical indications. Gleamer recorded a compound annual growth rate of over 90% in annual recurring revenue (ARR) from 2022 to 2025, with expectations to reach about $30 million ARR in 2026.

**Why it matters:** This is the first large-scale integration case directly linking AI automation to high-volume routine workflows (X-ray 25%, ultrasound, mammography) in the medical imaging industry. In an environment constrained by radiologist shortages and increasing imaging volumes, AI draft reporting and automatic triage enable maximization of radiologist reading productivity, marking entry into the stage of quantifying operational ROI.

- Gleamer portfolio: AI automated reporting for musculoskeletal, breast, lung, and neuro imaging + LiveRamp-style workflow automation, based on 4 FDA clearances, 6 CE marks, and 700 customer contracts in 44 countries
- DeepHealth integration synergy: Completes clinical AI portfolio covering all modalities including MR, CT, X-ray, mammography, and ultrasound, targeting over $140 million integrated ARR by end of 2026
- Expected operational efficiency gains for RadNet: Post-Q3 2026, deployment of automatic triage and draft reporting in high-volume X-ray areas expected to simultaneously increase readings per radiologist, improve standardization rates, and reduce operating costs

**What's next:** RadNet plans to combine Gleamer's cloud-native technology stack with DeepHealth's clinical AI integration capabilities to expand AI-prioritized workflows across all North American integrated imaging centers, with a wave of follow-on M&A expected among similar medical imaging companies.

**Source:** [RadNet - Acquires Gleamer, Making RadNet's DeepHealth the Largest Provider of Radiology Clinical AI Solutions Worldwide](https://www.radnet.com/about-radnet/news/radnet-acquires-gleamer-making-radnet-s-deephealth-the-largest-provider-of-radiology-clinical-ai-solutions-worldwide)
