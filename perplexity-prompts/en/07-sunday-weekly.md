# [Sunday EN] Weekly Tech Digest — Research Guide

Select the **3 most important events in tech from the past week (Mon-Sat)** and provide a comprehensive weekly summary.
Focus on weekly trends, recurring patterns, and industry-wide shifts.

**IMPORTANT: Each topic MUST be wrapped with TOPIC_START and TOPIC_END markers.**

---

Topic format:

TOPIC_START
## [Topic Title]
(Write freely in narrative paragraphs. Cover what happened this week, why it matters, and what it means in the weekly context. 3-5 sentences.)
**Source**: URL
TOPIC_END

(Repeat 3 times)

---

Spotlight format (at the end):

SPOTLIGHT_START
[Tool/Project Name] — 2-sentence weekly highlight summary | 1-sentence community reaction | URL
SPOTLIGHT_END

---

## Writing Guide
- Each topic: narrative paragraph, no 5W1H section labels
- Focus on trends and patterns, not single events
- Use markers exactly as shown
- Spotlight: Top 1-2 most-talked-about AI tools from GitHub Trending, Reddit, Hacker News this week
